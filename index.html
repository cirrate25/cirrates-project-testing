    Audio Gear Essentials

    🔊 Audio Interface

    The hub of your studio. Converts analog mic/guitar signals to digital and back. Without one, you get noise and latency.

    Start with: Focusrite Scarlett, Universal Audio

    🔈 Studio Monitors

    Flat frequency response — no bass boost or coloration. Hear what's really in your mix, not what your speakers want you to hear.

    Start with: KRK Rokit, Yamaha HS series

    🎧 Studio Headphones

    Closed-back for recording (no bleed into mic). Open-back for mixing (natural soundstage). Both essential.

    Start with: Audio-Technica ATH-M50x, Beyerdynamic DT 770/990

    🎤 Microphone

    Large-diaphragm condenser for vocals/acoustic. Dynamic (SM57/58) for amps/drums. Skip USB mics — go XLR through an interface.

    Start with: Audio-Technica AT2020, Shure SM57/SM58

    🔇 Soundproofing vs Acoustic Treatment

    Soundproofing

    Keeps sound in/out. Requires dense materials — drywall, mass-loaded vinyl. Expensive, often needs construction.

    Acoustic Treatment

    Controls reflections. Absorption panels at first-reflection points, bass traps in corners. Rugs, curtains, bookshelves help too.

    Quick tip: Record in a closet full of clothes for natural absorption. Use monitor stands to decouple from desk.

    📤 Posting — Distribution Getting your music on platforms

    To get your music on paid streaming platforms, you need a distributor. Here's what needs one and what doesn't.

    ℹ️ You Only Need Distributors For

    A distributor delivers your music to the major paid streaming platforms that don't accept direct uploads from artists. These are the platforms you need a distributor for:

    🎵 Spotify 🎵 Apple Music 🎵 Amazon Music 🎵 Tidal 🎵 Deezer 🎵 Pandora 🎵 TikTok Music 🎵 Napster 🎵 iHeartRadio

    You upload your music to the distributor once — they handle delivery to every platform, collect royalties, and pay you out.

    🌐 Platforms You Can Post To Without a Distributor

    These let you upload music directly — great for building an audience before you invest in distribution.

    🎬 YouTube 🎧 SoundCloud 🎵 Bandcamp 📸 Instagram / Facebook 🎤 TikTok 💿 Audius

    💡 The Smart Approach

    Start on SoundCloud, Bandcamp, and YouTube for free. Build an audience. Once you have traction and a few polished tracks, pick a distributor to get on Spotify and Apple Music. DistroKid is the best value for active releasers — unlimited uploads and 0% royalty cut at $24.99/year. If you want a single release and never pay again, CD Baby's one-time fee (9% royalty cut) is the better choice.

    Most important: read the fine print on what happens if you stop paying. DistroKid removes your catalog unless you buy Leave a Legacy. CD Baby keeps it up forever — but takes 9% forever too.
